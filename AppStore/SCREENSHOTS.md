# Screenshot set

Full-resolution captures of the shipping SwiftUI app on iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPad Pro 13-inch (M5). Store upload files are opaque JPEGs; original simulator PNGs are retained under `screenshots/raw`. No UI features, accuracy results or platform integrations have been drawn into the images.

The lecture and meeting records are clearly labelled **דוגמה**. Their text and durations are composed demonstration content for the library/editor, not measured recognition output. The app does not claim automatic punctuation or summaries. `screenshot-fixtures.json` and `scripts/prepare_screenshot_simulator.py` reproduce and safely remove only these fixtures. They are not bundled into the app.

Recommended order:

| 5 | Privacy settings | במכשיר שלך. גם בלי אינטרנט. |

The native captures are uploadable without overlays. Optional future slots: editing, TXT export, audio playback, Shortcuts and recording Live Activity. Capture those actual system flows before adding screenshots; avoid a WhatsApp-specific promise before a phone test. Ten images are unnecessary for this focused first release.

Palette: warm off-white background, burnt-orange action color, dark charcoal Hebrew text. Use system Hebrew sans-serif and RTL alignment if adding editorial artwork. Do not obscure actual controls or add fabricated badges. No preview video is needed for the initial utility listing.
